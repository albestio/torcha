# Code of conduct

## Our commitment

This project is a place to do careful work in public. Everyone who takes part —
in issues, in pull requests, in review — is expected to make it possible for
others to do the same, regardless of experience, language, identity or the
number of questions they need to ask.

## What is expected

- **Argue about the work, not the person.** "This breaks the case where the tab
  was renamed" is a review. "You clearly did not think" is not.
- **Say what you know and what you assumed.** A wrong assumption stated clearly
  is a fixable thing; one hidden behind confident prose is a bug.
- **Respect the language of the room.** Discussion happens in whatever language
  the participants share; code, messages and documentation are English.
- **Be patient with a question you consider basic.** You were new to this once,
  and someone was patient with you.

## What is not acceptable

Harassment, insults, sexualised attention, deliberate intimidation, publishing
someone's private information, and sustained disruption of a discussion.

## Scope

This applies in every space this project uses: issues, pull requests, reviews and
any private channel opened to discuss a report.

## Enforcement

The maintainer reads and acts on reports. Report a problem through the
repository's maintainer contact rather than in a public thread, and include what
happened and where. Reports are handled confidentially; the person reporting will
be told what happened as a result, unless doing so would expose someone else.

Responses range from a private word to closing a thread to blocking an account
from the project, in proportion to what happened and whether it continues.
